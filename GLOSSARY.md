# Advent 2026

A family companion to BibleProject's Advent 2026 daily calendar: one short Scripture reading and reflection question per day, from Sunday, November 29 through Christmas Day.

## The calendar

**Calendar**:
One year's set of Advent content (intro, Weeks, Days), as published by BibleProject.
_Avoid_: Plan, schedule

**Week**:
One of the four seven-day stretches of Advent, each named for its Theme. The fourth Week ends on Christmas Day and has only six Days.

**Theme**:
The word that names a Week: Hope, Peace, Joy or Love.
_Avoid_: Topic, candle

**Day**:
One dated entry in the Calendar, with a Reading and a Reflection Question.
_Avoid_: Entry, devotional, door

**Reading**:
The Scripture reference for a Day (e.g. "Romans 8:20–21"), opened in Logos.
_Avoid_: Verse (a Reading can span several verses or part of one), passage

**Reflection Question**:
The question a Day asks the family to discuss after the Reading.
_Avoid_: Prompt, discussion question

**Video**:
The BibleProject video that goes with a Week's Theme.

## Taking part

**Done**:
A Day the family has marked as finished on a given device.
_Avoid_: Read, complete, checked

**Reminder**:
A daily notification on a device, at a time chosen on that device, pointing to that day's Day.
_Avoid_: Alert, nudge
